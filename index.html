Scoring rules reference
Maze completion (50 pts, cumulative): 10 pts at the solid line, +15 at the dashed line, +25 at the exit.
Wall collisions: −2 pts each, subtracted from the maze completion points.
Time score (50 pts, only if the maze is completed): ≤1 min = 50, ≤2 min = 40, ≤3 min = 30, ≤4 min = 20, ≤5 min = 10, over 5 min = 0.
Attempt penalty: attempts 1–2 score at 100%, attempt 3 at 75%, attempt 4 at 50% — applied to the maze + time subtotal.
TL sticker bonus: flat +10, added after the attempt penalty.
Ultrasonic-only challenge: if navigation is done entirely without IR, the final score (including the sticker bonus) is doubled.
This calculator applies the multiplier order above. If your event scores attempts in a different order, treat the line items in the breakdown as a reference and adjust manually.
